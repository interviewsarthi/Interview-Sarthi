Interview Sarthi vs Final Round AI

Quick answer: for one or two interviews on a Windows laptop, especially in Hinglish, Interview Sarthi costs less: 30 minutes free, then unlimited use for ₹99 (2 days) or ₹299 (30 days) in India ($9.99 and $29.99 elsewhere), one-time. Final Round AI suits you if you interview from a Mac or want its wider preparation suite; its live Interview Copilot is in the Pro plan, from $25 a month.

Disclosure: we build Interview Sarthi. Public information reviewed on 15 September 2026; Final Round AI's pricing page rechecked on 2 October 2026. This page is not a hands-on benchmark.

What we could verify

Final Round AI: its pricing page (checked 2 October 2026) lists a free plan without the live Interview Copilot, and a Pro plan from $25 a month ("$25+/mo") that includes unlimited Copilot sessions; the exact amount depends on the billing period you choose. Its desktop app runs on macOS 14.4+ and Windows. Official pricing.

The official site offers interview preparation and a live Interview Copilot.

Interview Sarthi offers 30 minutes free per computer, then unlimited use for ₹99 (2 days) or ₹299 (30 days) in India ($9.99 and $29.99 elsewhere). Passes do not auto-renew. See the pricing cards and product facts.

What changes the decision

If structured practice is your priority, inspect the practice workflow separately from live assistance; request current session limits before comparing costs.

Interview Sarthi focuses on resume-grounded assistance on Windows, in English, Hindi and Hinglish. You provide a Gemini key and set up the app yourself. It can assist during a mock call with another person, but it does not provide an autonomous mock interviewer or resume builder. If those are essential, evaluate a preparation suite.

Compare total cost for your own schedule

List the calls you expect, their likely length and the days between rounds. A calendar-day pass expires with time; a metered plan uses credits or minutes. Check the selected vendor plan for expiration, per-call limits, device limits, renewal and refunds. Do not compare a short pass with a monthly subscription as if they cover the same period.

For Interview Sarthi, the 2-day pass covers one device; the 30-day pass covers two. Google API limits still apply, and enabling paid API use creates a separate Google cost.

Run a fair mock-call trial

  1. Use the same computer, headset and call platform where possible.
  2. Ask the same project question and follow-up; check whether the answer matches your resume.
  3. Try a mixed Hindi-English sentence and verify names, numbers and technical terms.
  4. Record errors and delays rather than relying on a single fluent answer.

We have not verified Final Round AI's Hinglish accuracy, data path or behavior on every meeting platform. A missing verification is not a claim that a feature is absent. Review its current privacy and support documentation.
