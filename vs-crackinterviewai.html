Who each one suits

CrackInterviewAI suits one short interview if 30 minutes is enough: ₹99. Its pricing page notes that checkout amounts may differ from its table.

Live Sarthi costs less for more time: ₹149 covers 2 days with no hour limit, where CrackInterviewAI's ₹349 buys 3 hours; and ₹299 covers 30 days on 2 PCs, where CrackInterviewAI's monthly unlimited plan is ₹2,499 on one PC. It answers in English, Hindi or Hinglish, from your own resume, in about 1 second, and uses your own free Google Gemini key.

Our verdict

Both give 30 free minutes: test both on the same practice call. For more than one short call, Live Sarthi's passes cost less in the prices both pages showed on the date above. Use any assistant only where the interview's rules allow help.
