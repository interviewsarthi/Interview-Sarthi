Refund Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026 · Interview Sarthi is operated by an individual seller in India. Payments from India are processed by Cashfree Payments. Payments from outside India are sold through Dodo Payments Inc as merchant of record, which was also the case for every order placed before 16 September 2026.

Try it free before you pay

Live Sarthi gives you 30 free minutes and Prep Sarthi a free demo interview, with every feature included. Please test them on the computer or phone, the browser and the call software you will actually use before buying a pass. Because you can try everything first, we do not refund a pass because you changed your mind or did not like the answers.

Refunds: only if it does not work on your device, within 2 days

We refund a Live Sarthi or Prep Sarthi pass in full only when all three of these are true:

  • It does not work on your device. Live Sarthi will not install, open or accept your key, cannot hear the interviewer, or gives no answers. Prep Sarthi will not start or run an interview on your device.
  • You tell us within 2 days (48 hours) of purchase, through Help in the app, the chat on this site or support@interviewsarthi.com, with the email address you paid with and what happened.
  • We could not fix it with you. Most problems are solved by the steps on the help page or a quick reply from us.

How it works

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method, through Cashfree Payments for a payment made in India and through Dodo Payments otherwise, usually within 5–7 working days.
  • The license key or pass is disabled when the refund is issued.
  • We do not refund a pass after 2 days from purchase, for a change of mind, for answers you did not like, for an interview that was cancelled or moved, or for days you did not use. Passes do not auto-renew, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • Orders paid through Dodo Payments (outside India): Dodo Payments is the seller of record, its Buyer Terms also apply, and Dodo makes the final decision on a refund.
  • Passes bought before 19 October 2026 keep the refund terms that were shown when they were bought.

Chargebacks

Please email us first. A refund request is answered faster than a bank dispute, and a dispute filed without contacting us disables the pass immediately.
