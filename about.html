About Interview Sarthi

Three apps for finding jobs, practising interviews and getting live interview assistance. This page explains who builds them, what each one does and how to contact us.

Who builds this

Interview Sarthi is built and supported by an independent software developer in India, not a company with a sales team. The same person who writes the code answers the support mail. Live Sarthi’s Windows releases are published openly on GitHub, so every installer you download is a file with a public version history, not a mystery download.

Three apps for your job search

  • Apply Sarthi — find and apply to jobs. Search jobs from multiple portals and career boards, match roles to your CV and use the Chrome extension to help fill supported application forms. Always free.
  • Prep Sarthi — practise an AI mock interview. Answer by voice from your CV and target role, practise follow-up questions and review feedback on weak answers. Runs in your browser. A free 7-minute demo, then ₹99 for 30 days of unlimited mock interviews.
  • Live Sarthi — get live interview answers. A Windows assistant that uses the question and your resume to suggest answers. Replies start in about 1.5 seconds under suitable conditions; speed varies. It stays hidden in supported Windows screen captures. 30 minutes free, then unlimited passes from ₹99 for 2 days.

Why we built Interview Sarthi

Job seekers spend time switching between job sites, finding someone to practise with and working out how to answer under pressure. Each app addresses one of those tasks. You can use just the app you need: search with Apply, practise for the role with Prep, or get assistance with Live where the interviewer permits it.

“Sarthi” (सारथी) means charioteer or guide. Our aim is practical help at a price a job seeker can try. The apps do not guarantee an interview or an offer.

How the business works

Apply is always free. Prep and Live offer free trials and separate paid passes for fixed periods. Passes do not auto-renew. Live and a Prep pass use your own Gemini API key (Prep's free demo needs none); Google’s availability and usage limits are separate from an Interview Sarthi pass. See all product plans and the refund policy.

Accounts and privacy differ by app

Apply uses an account and backend services. Prep uses your Gemini key for practice, with Google sign-in for paid access. Live uses a device trial and licence activation. Payment and support services also handle the details needed to deliver those services. Read the privacy policy for the data handled by each app and its providers.
