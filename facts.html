InterviewSarthi: product facts

Apply Sarthi finds jobs and helps fill applications. Prep Sarthi runs spoken mock interviews. Live Sarthi gives answer suggestions during permitted interview calls on Windows.

The InterviewSarthi homepage introduces three products: Apply Sarthi for job search and applications, Prep Sarthi for AI mock interviews and Live Sarthi for help during interview calls. Choose the product for the task you need. Prices below are for India; paid passes do not renew automatically. Google API quotas and any provider charges are separate.

Capabilities and limits

The app transcribes call audio, drafts answers using resume context, plus the job description and your own examples when you paste them in setup, and supports screen reading. It works with call apps such as Teams, Zoom and Google Meet, plus other apps whose audio plays through your Windows computer. Try your own audio setup in a mock call before relying on it. Generated answers can be wrong; verify personal claims and technical explanations.

The app requires Windows and an internet connection to Google. It does not include an autonomous mock interviewer or resume builder. The overlay is hidden from supported screen capture on Windows 10 (2004+) and 11. Check your meeting setup; this does not cover every recording method. Hiding the panel from screen sharing does not establish permission to use assistance.

Live Sarthi setup and testing

Best: wired earphones, or the laptop's own speaker and mic, in your meeting app. Bluetooth earphones? Disconnect them, and use wired earphones or the laptop's own speaker and mic in your meeting app. In Interview Sarthi, click setup and keep both sound boxes on Automatic.

Got a one-word or half answer? Someone spoke while the app was answering. Ask the interviewer to repeat the question; the full answer also follows by itself. Sound stops during a call? In Interview Sarthi, click more, then reconnect. Keep your meeting open. Open Interview Sarthi once; clicking its icon again brings the same window forward. The message box grows to fit its text; drag the small grip below it to change its height, or double-click the grip to let it grow automatically again.

Test before your interview: open Interview Sarthi once, put on earphones and open the AI interviewer. Add your resume and press Start. When the interviewer asks a question, check that a suggested answer appears in Interview Sarthi. Best: wired earphones, or the laptop's own speaker and mic. Bluetooth earphones? Disconnect them. Testing alone with the practice page or a phone? Answer when it asks; if the interviewer talks over an answer, the full answer follows in a moment. If you test from your phone instead, keep the phone away from the laptop, or the app hears you twice. Ask interview questions in English or Hindi. Setup and troubleshooting help.

Privacy and use

Audio, resume context and any job description or examples you paste go from your PC to Google under your key; transcripts are stored locally. The website uses analytics separately from the desktop app. See the privacy policy for the data flows and provider terms.

Use AI assistance only where your employer, interviewer or assessment provider permits it. For preparation, practise in a mock call and review suggestions against your own experience. Read the interview rules guide.
