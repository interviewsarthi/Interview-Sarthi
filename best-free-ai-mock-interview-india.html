Best free AI mock interview in India (2026): 9 tools checked

Quick answer: for spoken practice built from your own CV and target job, on a phone or laptop, in English, Hindi or Hinglish, with no sign-up: start with Prep Sarthi's free 7-minute mock interview, then ₹99 once for 30 days. For free practice with no limit, Huru is free for job seekers (you log in; English and four European languages). For Hindi or Hinglish drills by topic, Hirenix gives free voice mocks of 5 questions each.

Publisher disclosure: we build Prep Sarthi. This is an editorial comparison of each tool's official pages, checked on 11 October 2026. It is not an independent benchmark of interview quality.

Quick picks by need

  • Practise answers about your own CV and projects, out loud, today: Prep Sarthi (our product). A free 7-minute spoken demo with no sign-up, no card and no API key; it works in a phone browser. After that, ₹99 once for 30 days ($9.99 outside India), with Google sign-in and your own free Gemini key. Of the tools here, it is the only one whose official page says a spoken mock needs no sign-up.
  • Unlimited free practice in English: Huru. Free for job seekers with unlimited mocks; you answer on camera. Web, iPhone app and Chrome extension. It lists English, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French, not Hindi.
  • Hindi, Hinglish or a regional language, by topic: Hirenix. Free voice mocks of 5 questions; Pro is ₹99 for 30 days, one-time or auto-renewing. Its app runs in English, Hinglish, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Marathi. Its pricing page does not say the questions use your CV.
  • Speaking style and delivery: Yoodli. 5 sessions free for life (any session over 30 seconds counts); Pro $8 a month billed annually.
  • Lowest monthly price with Hindi: Cloudvyn. 5 free interviews a month; Plus ₹50 a month by UPI Autopay, with unlimited interviews if you bring your own AI key.
  • Pay per interview: MockMind. 2 free 5-minute interviews per account, then ₹39 for one or ₹99 for three.

What about Google Interview Warmup?

Many lists still name Google Interview Warmup. On 11 October 2026 its old address redirected to a general interview-tips article on Grow with Google, so we have left it out of the table.

How to choose

  • Interview in the next few days: practise the questions you will really get, about your own CV and projects, out loud. A tool that builds questions from your CV and the job description is closer to the real round than a generic question bank.
  • Hinglish interviews: check that the tool understands technical English inside a Hindi sentence, not just a separate Hindi mode. See our Hinglish interview answers guide.
  • Only a phone: check that the mock runs in a phone browser or has an app before you sign up. Some tools recommend a laptop.
  • Subscriptions: a one-time pass ends on its own; an auto-renewing plan keeps charging until you cancel.
