Questions people ask

Which AI mock interview is free?

Huru is free for job seekers with unlimited mocks; Hirenix gives unlimited 5-question voice mocks; Internshala gives 5 full mock interviews a month; BiasZero is free with unlimited retakes. Prep Sarthi's first 7-minute mock interview is free with no sign-up (checked 12 November 2026).

Which mock interview uses my own CV?

Prep Sarthi builds its questions from your CV and the job you want. Internshala personalises its questions to a job description, MockMind takes a resume upload, and ClearRound uses your CV and the job description.

How much does a paid AI mock interview cost in India?

Per session, HireStepX is ₹9, or ₹39 for 5 sessions, and MockMind is ₹39 for one interview. Prep Sarthi is ₹99 for 30 days of unlimited mock interviews; Hirenix Pro is ₹99 for 30 days; ClearRound's pass is ₹499 for 7 days.

Can I practise on my phone?

Prep Sarthi runs in a phone browser. Huru has an iPhone app and a web app. Some tools, such as BiasZero, say they work best on a laptop.
