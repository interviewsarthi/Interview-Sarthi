Practise the Infosys interview out loud

Practise explaining your own projects and answering follow-up questions before your Infosys interview. Add your CV and the actual job description, then speak your answers out loud and review the feedback.

Independent practice, based on your CV. Prep Sarthi is not affiliated with Infosys. It creates practice questions from your CV and role; it does not reproduce an official interview or predict the questions you will be asked.

What to practise

Prepare forWhat to do
Your hiring routeRead the role, test instructions and interview invitation. Campus hiring and competition-based hiring can differ.
Technical discussionPractise explaining your project decisions, OOP, SQL and the programming skills in the job description.
HR discussionPrepare an introduction, a specific reason for applying and honest answers about availability and location.

Explain your project beyond its summary

Say what problem you worked on, what you personally built, why you chose that approach and what you would change. Be ready to support any numbers on your CV.

Practise a follow-up without starting again

If asked for more detail, add a decision, example or test result. Repeating the opening answer does not fill the gap. Use the feedback report to choose one weak answer for your next practice session.

A 20-minute practice plan for Infosys

Use your free demo to find one or two answers that need work. This is a suggested practice plan, not an estimate of the employer’s interview duration.

  1. Upload your CV and add the Infosys job description if you have it.
  2. Start on your project and the core subjects, and answer as you would in the technical round.
  3. When the questions turn personal, let your delivery change with them. Warmer, slower, first person.
  4. Have a real answer for why Infosys. One specific thing, not three general ones.
  5. Check the report for your words per minute. People speed up when they are uncomfortable, and the HR round is where that shows.

Questions people ask

Does this reproduce the Infosys interview?

No. Prep Sarthi creates practice questions from your CV and target role. It is independent of Infosys; questions, round structure and hiring decisions come from the employer.

What should I upload?

Use the CV you plan to submit and the actual job description, if available. Check that your project details and dates are accurate before starting.

What should I do with the feedback?

Review the weak answers, check suggested facts against your own experience and practise those answers again. AI feedback can be wrong and is not an employer score.
