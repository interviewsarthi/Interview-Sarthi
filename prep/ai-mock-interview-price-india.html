How to compare value for your interview date

  1. Count useful practice time. A credit can mean a question, a voice session or a shared allowance across several tools. Check the session length and reset period.
  2. Check the questions and feedback. Try a role you know. See whether the interviewer uses your CV and job description, asks useful follow-ups and explains how to improve an answer.
  3. Check the payment period. For one interview this week, compare short access passes. For repeated practice, compare the full monthly or annual charge and cancellation terms.
  4. Include AI-provider costs. A bring-your-own-key app charges for its software separately. A free software trial does not remove Google’s model availability, quotas or paid-use charges.

AI practice or a human mock interview?

AI can make repeated spoken practice easier to arrange. A human interviewer may offer judgement, specialist experience and a conversation that an AI cannot reliably reproduce. They are different services, so this page does not claim one costs a fixed multiple of the other. For a human option, InterviewBuddy describes expert sessions alongside its AI practice; check the current quote for your role and duration.
