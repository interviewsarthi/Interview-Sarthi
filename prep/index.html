What it will not do

An interviewer, not a flatterer.

It never coaches you mid-interview.

During the call she is an interviewer, not a tutor. The teaching is in the report, where it belongs.

It never invents facts about you.

Questions come from your CV. Stronger answers use only what your CV and your own words established.

It never runs over.

The interview ends the second your time is up, and the report is written from what was covered.

It never keeps your CV or your voice.

Google's Gemini runs the interviewer. In the free demo, the requests that prepare your interview and write your report pass through our server on their way to Google and are not stored; with a pass, everything goes straight from your browser to Google. Reports stay in your browser unless you choose to keep them in your account.

Questions people ask

Short answers.

What does it cost?

Your first mock is free: a 7-minute demo interview with your full scored report, with no card, no sign-up and nothing to set up. After that a pass gives you unlimited mocks: Rs 99 for 30 days. One payment through UPI, card or netbanking, and it never renews by itself. We also published AI mock interview prices and free trials, so you can check.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It is a web page, not an app to install. It needs a browser with a microphone. Earphones help, because from a loud speaker the interviewer can hear her own voice.

Which languages can the interview be in?

English, Hinglish and Hindi are one tap. Type any other language, or choose "match whatever I speak" and she follows you.

Can I have a second free demo?

The demo is one per person. After it, a pass gives you unlimited mock interviews for 30 days.

Can I get free days by inviting friends?

Yes: give a week, get a week. After your demo you get a link to share. A friend who buys a pass through it gets 37 days instead of 30, and you get 7 free days for every friend who buys, even if you have not bought a pass yourself. Your free days wait until you start them.

She stopped talking for a few seconds. Is it broken?

Google's free tier sometimes goes quiet mid-call. Prep Sarthi notices, reconnects by itself and carries on from the same question. Your transcript and timer are kept.

My PDF did not read properly.

A scanned CV is a picture, so there is no text to read. Paste the text into the box instead. It works just as well.

How is this different from Live Sarthi?

Prep Sarthi is for the days before: it plays the interviewer so you can practise. Live Sarthi is for the day itself: a Windows app that helps you during the real call.
