Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026 · Interview Sarthi is operated by an individual seller in India. Payments from India are processed by Cashfree Payments. Payments from outside India are sold through Dodo Payments Inc as merchant of record, which was also the case for every order placed before 16 September 2026.

Scope: this policy covers Live Sarthi, Prep Sarthi, My Sarthi (the account page) and this website. Live interview content goes from your PC to Google; Prep interview content goes from your browser to Google, and is kept in your Prep Sarthi account only if you switch on saved interviews; in the free demo, the requests that prepare the interview and write the report pass through our license server on the way, without being stored. We operate licensing and payment services as described below. Apply Sarthi stores account data under its separate privacy policy.

What Live Sarthi processes, and where

  • Meeting audio: captured on your PC and streamed to Google Gemini under your own API key for transcription and answers. It is not sent to us.
  • Your resume: converted to text on your PC and stored in %APPDATA%\Interview Sarthi. A compressed summary is sent to the AI provider with each answer request. A scanned PDF is sent once to Gemini to be read.
  • Job description and your own examples, only if you type them in setup: stored as text in the same folder on your PC and sent to the AI provider as written, alongside the resume summary. Empty a box and press Save to delete its file.
  • Transcripts and summaries: saved on your PC. Delete the folder above to remove them. If you connect Live Sarthi to My Sarthi and switch on "Keep my Live interviews" there, your interviews from the past year on that PC (sent once) and each finished interview after that are also kept in your My Sarthi account (see My Sarthi below).
  • API keys: stored on your PC in config.env. Never transmitted to us, unless you save your Gemini key in My Sarthi yourself (see My Sarthi below).

What we receive

  • Purchase data: when you buy a pass, your email address and phone number are collected at checkout and stored in our own licensing database with the plan and the order status. Your card, UPI and netbanking details go to Cashfree Payments and are never seen by us. Passes bought from outside India, and every pass bought before 16 September 2026, are sold by Dodo Payments Inc under their privacy policy.
  • License checks: on start-up and about every 10 minutes during a session, the software sends your license key and a device name (for example “DESKTOP-3F2 (Windows)”) to confirm the pass is valid. Keys beginning “IS-” go to our own license server at license.interviewsarthi.com and also carry an anonymous device id, so reinstalling on the same PC takes back the slot it already had. Keys issued before 16 September 2026 are checked against Dodo Payments. Nothing about the interview is included. Since 3 October 2026 our license server also notes, for each “IS-” key, the days on which these checks arrived and whether the pass was still valid, so we can see how passes are used, for example whether the app is opened again after a pass has ended. That note holds the key, the date, how many checks came and the times of the first and last, and nothing else. From version 1.0.41 each check also carries the app version and, when our server could not be reached for a while, how many minutes the app ran in that time.
  • Update check: on start-up the software downloads one small file from this website that says what the newest version is, so it can show which version you have and which is the newest, and an "update" button with the steps when yours is older. That request carries nothing about you or your PC.
  • Update minutes: from version 1.0.40, the first time a new version starts on a PC without an active pass, the software asks our license server whether that PC gets 20 free minutes to try it. That request carries the anonymous device id, the app version, how many minutes are asked for and your key if it begins “IS-”; while you use those minutes, the software tells the server how many seconds have been used. Nothing about your interview is included.
  • Help and problem reports, only when you send them: the app's help window sends us your problem only when you click it. With it go a short summary of the app's own checks (app and Windows version, whether your Gemini key is saved and works, your license state and license key, your sound devices, and numbers from your last session such as how long it ran and how many lines were heard) and the app's logs from your last few sessions — these include the transcribed lines of what was said — your audio device names and drivers, how far the app got at start-up, and your settings with every key removed. Your resume, the audio recordings and your Gemini key are never included. A first answer is written automatically from our help page and that summary; to write it, your message and the summary (never the transcribed lines) may go to Google's Gemini. If a person is needed, the Sarthi team reads your problem, through Telegram, and replies in the app. Add your email if you would also like the reply by email when the app is closed, with a link to the conversation. While a conversation is open, the app checks for replies about once a minute, sending only the conversation's number and how far you have read. We keep the conversation to help you and to improve the app; the log files are deleted after 30 days. If our support chat is not reachable, the problem and logs come to us as an email report instead.
  • Reports from the end of an interview, only when you send them: when you answer "No" or "Partly" to "Did Live Sarthi help in this interview?", or a session ends without any answers, the end screen offers a Send report button. Pressing it sends the same summary and logs as a help report (listed above), plus your answer, the words you typed under it and the session's date and time, and opens a help conversation in the app. These logs are kept on our own license server and deleted after 30 days. The Sarthi team's reply shows in the app's help window; if your pass was bought with an email address, it is also emailed there. Without a pass you can type an email address for the reply. Nothing is sent unless you press the button.
  • Your feedback, only when you give it: at the end of an interview the app asks whether Live Sarthi helped. Nothing is sent unless you click an answer; then that answer, the app version and the anonymous device id go to our server, with your key if it begins “IS-”, and any words you type below it go too when you press Send. After “No” or “Partly” you can also tick what went wrong (for example “It didn't hear the interviewer”); each tick is sent the same way. From version 1.0.40 your answer also carries numbers about that interview, never its words: how long it ran, how many questions were heard and answered, how many you typed, how quickly answers came, which AI model answered and how often the connection dropped or the model changed, how much of the time each side was silent, whether a Bluetooth headset was in call mode, whether a resume, a job description and your own examples were set up (yes or no only, never their text), the interview language and your answer-style choices. No question, answer, transcript line or typed word from the interview is included. The day after a pass ends we send one email asking how your interview went; what you answer on the page it links to (whether it helped, whether you have had another interview since, what went wrong, what we should change) is kept with your pass. We read every answer to improve the app, and never publish them.
  • Your email address, if you give it: setup has an optional email box. Fill it in and the address alone is sent to our server, so we can send your license key if you buy a pass and tell you when features land. Leave it empty and nothing is sent. No account is created either way, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Support email: whatever you choose to send us, including any problem report you attach.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to measure visits and page interactions. They do not run inside the Live Sarthi desktop app. Prep uses Google Analytics for the events described in its section below; Clarity is disabled inside the Prep app and on receipt pages. Block these tools with a content blocker if you prefer; the website still works. Read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Website measurement includes an AI referral event when a recognised campaign source or referring hostname is present. It records the source category and landing-page path, without query strings. Receipt pages with license keys are excluded from Clarity recording, and receipt query values are removed before analytics loads.

The chat assistant

The Sarthi chat bubble on this website and on ApplySarthi answers questions about our apps. It is an AI assistant: it cannot see orders, payments, license keys or accounts, and it never asks for them.

What we keep. The messages you send and the replies, the page you sent them from, whether you appear to be in India (from your browser's time zone), and a random identifier your browser creates so a later chat can pick up where the last one ended. We also keep a one-line summary of each chat, written by the same AI. If you tell the assistant your first name, it remembers it. If you answer "where did you hear about us", leave a review or a rating, or use the form that sends a problem to the team, we keep what you entered there, including the email address and order ID you give in that form; reviews and problems are also sent to the owner by Telegram. We do not keep your network address: limits against abuse use a one-way hash of it. License keys, Gemini keys and card numbers typed into the chat are removed before anything is stored or sent. Chats are kept to improve the assistant and our apps, and are not deleted automatically. Please do not share passwords or keys in the chat.

Who processes it. To write each reply, your message, the recent chat and text from our website are sent to Google's Gemini through Google Cloud (Vertex AI), where Google does not use them to train its models. If Google Cloud is unavailable, a reply may instead be written on Google's free Gemini API, under which Google may use what it receives to improve its services. Your browser keeps the identifier and your last 40 messages so the chat follows you between pages; clearing site data removes them.

To see or delete your chats, email us and say roughly when you chatted and what about, or give the email address you entered in the chat.

Prep Sarthi

Prep Sarthi, the mock interviewer at interviewsarthi.com/prep, runs in your browser. The report is saved in your browser's storage, where clearing site data removes it. With a pass you can also choose to keep your interviews in your account, as described under Saved interviews below.

The free demo. Your first mock interview runs on Interview Sarthi's own Gemini account, so you need nothing of your own. Your voice and the interviewer's replies travel between your browser and Google directly, using a one-time access token our license server obtains from Google for that interview. The request that prepares the interview (your CV and the job description) and the request that writes your report (the interview transcript) pass through our license server on their way to Google; we do not store or log their contents. The demo uses the free tier of Google's Gemini API, under which Google may use what it receives to improve its services. To keep the demo to one per person, we store a random identifier your browser creates for this purpose, a one-way hash of your network address (never the address itself), when the demo started and how many requests it made.

With a pass. Your CV, the job description, your voice and the interviewer's replies travel between your browser and Google's Gemini on your own API key. They do not pass through our servers on the way, and unless you switch on saved interviews (below), or save your CV and job description to My Sarthi yourself, we do not store them. The report is written the same way.

Saved interviews (optional, with a pass). Nothing is saved to your account until you switch this on in the app. When it is on, each finished interview is kept in your Prep Sarthi account on our license server, so you can follow your progress across your pass and open any report on any device where you sign in. We keep the report, the interview transcript (what you and the interviewer said, as text), the delivery numbers worked out from it (such as your pace and pauses), and the short quotes from your CV and job description that the report cites; also the analyses of your progress and the answers you practise again, which are built from those. Saved interviews never include your CV or your job description themselves (those are stored only if you save them to My Sarthi yourself), and never your voice. The analyses are written by Google's Gemini on your own key, from your saved interviews, the same way as the reports. Only you, signed in to your account, can read what is saved. You can delete one interview or everything at any time in the app; switching saving off stops new saves and deletes nothing. Everything saved is deleted automatically 12 months after the interview it belongs to, and it stays readable after your pass ends until then.

Where free minutes on your own key apply, the page counts them by sending our license server a one-way SHA-256 hash of your Gemini key and the number of seconds used. The key itself never leaves your browser, unless you save it to My Sarthi yourself (see My Sarthi), and cannot be recovered from the hash. We store that hash, the seconds used, and the first and last time it was seen. Nothing else about you or your interview is sent to us, and no account is needed.

Invites. Your invite code is tied to this browser (after the free demo) or to your key. If you arrive through a friend's link, we store which code you came through, so that if you buy a pass your first one lasts 37 days and your friend gets 7 free days. The friend who invited you sees only how many friends bought a pass, never who they are.

Passes. To buy a pass you sign in with Google. We receive your Google account's email address, name and Google's identifier for the account, and store them with the time your pass ends. Buying from India you also enter a mobile number, because Cashfree requires one; buying from anywhere else you do not, and we store none. Whichever processor takes the payment, we store the order with your email, the plan and that processor’s payment reference, and email you a receipt. A sign-in session is kept in your browser's storage for up to 30 days. Passes never renew by themselves, so no payment details are kept by us or charged again.

Your Gemini key is kept in your browser's storage only if you leave "Remember on this device" ticked. The Prep Sarthi app page counts visits and steps with Google Analytics (for example that an interview started, its language and length, and the report score) and never records the screen: Microsoft Clarity is switched off inside the app. Google processes what you send it under its own terms; on Google's free tier it may use that content to improve its products, which is Google's rule for free keys and applies to anything you do with one.

Interview reliability logs. For demo and other interview attempts, we store a random attempt identifier and operational events for 30 days: microphone checks, preparation and connection stages, disconnects, timeouts, retries, model or provider fallbacks, report success or failure, timing, and token counts when supplied by the AI provider. Server events can include the demo identifier and an anonymous key slot, never the key itself. These logs do not contain audio, CV or job-description text, transcripts, generated answers, names, email addresses, or raw error messages. A hashed network identifier limits abuse; we do not store the IP address in these logs. Unsynced diagnostic events may remain in your browser for up to 24 hours so temporary connection failures can be investigated. This operational logging is separate from optional saved interview history.

My Sarthi, your account page

My Sarthi, at interviewsarthi.com/account, shows in one place what you have with us. You sign in with Google, the same way as in Prep Sarthi; we receive the name and email address of that Google account. The page shows your Live Sarthi passes (matched by the email address you bought them with, and any key you add by hand), the PCs each pass is used on, what you bought, your support conversations, your invite code, and your Prep Sarthi pass and saved interviews. Reading them makes no new copy of them. The bell on the page shows our news and reminders about your own passes, support replies and newly connected PCs; it sends no email and no phone notification.

What My Sarthi can store, why, and for how long. Everything below is stored only when you do it yourself; nothing is copied in on its own. It is kept in our database on Cloudflare (hosted in the Asia-Pacific region) and read only by you, signed in, and by the PCs and apps you connect, except where this policy says otherwise.

  • Your profile, if you save it: your resume as text, up to ten job descriptions, your own examples, your name, language, target role and level. Purpose: Live Sarthi and Prep Sarthi fill their empty setup boxes from it, so you type them once. Kept until you delete any part, or the whole profile, on the page.
  • Your Gemini API key, if you save it: stored encrypted. Purpose: the same, so a PC or browser you connect can use your key without you pasting it again. It is only ever sent back to you, to your own browser or to a PC you connected; we never use it ourselves. Remove it on the page at any time.
  • Connected PCs: when you press Connect in Live Sarthi's setup, the app shows a code and opens this page; nothing is linked until you type that code here yourself and press Connect. We keep the PC's name, the app version and when it was connected and last used. A connected PC can read your profile (to fill its empty boxes) and, with the choice below, send your interviews. Disconnect any PC on the page or in the app. Only type a code you can see on your own screen: never one that someone sent you.
  • Kept Live interviews, only if you tick "Keep my Live interviews": this is off until you tick it. With it on, each connected PC sends your account the interviews from the past year that are already on it, once, and after that each interview when it ends: what the app heard from the call (on most setups the interviewer; if your headset feeds your own voice into the call's sound, that can be part of it), the screen text the app read, and every answer it showed you, with the date, length, language and app version. The app does not transcribe your microphone for this (lines that older versions of the app took from it are left out), and no audio recording or screenshot is ever sent. Purpose: so you can read your interviews again on any device. Each is deleted automatically after 365 days; delete any of them, or all, on the page at any time, and that PC does not send them again. Switching this off stops new interviews being sent and deletes nothing. Each account can keep about 10 MB.
  • Reading them to improve the app, only if you also tick that choice: a separate, optional tick lets Sarthi read your kept interviews, not linked to your account's name or email, to improve Live Sarthi's answers (for example which questions come up and where an answer fell short). Keeping your interviews works without this. Untick it at any time; from then on we do not read anything new of yours.
  • Your choices: we keep a record of each time you switch either choice on or off, with the date and the version of this text you saw, as the law asks us to.

Your rights and questions. You can see, change and delete everything above on the page itself. For anything else, including to object or complain, write to support@interviewsarthi.com; we answer within a few days, and in any case within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to India's Data Protection Board once it is open to complaints.

ApplySarthi

Apply Sarthi, at apply.interviewsarthi.com, has its own account and privacy policy. That policy explains storage of CVs, application documents and encrypted Gemini keys. This page covers Live Sarthi, Prep Sarthi and this website.

What we do not do

No ads or selling of personal data. The Live Sarthi desktop app has no usage analytics; website and Prep measurement are described above. Apart from the license check, the update check, the update-minutes request, the optional email box and, while a support conversation is open, its check for replies, all described above, the desktop app sends us nothing on its own: no analytics, no usage events, and nothing about your interview, with one exception that you choose: a PC you connected to My Sarthi with "Keep my Live interviews" switched on sends its interviews from the past year once and then each finished interview, as described under My Sarthi. A problem goes to us only when you send it from the help window or with the Send report button at the end of an interview, and what each contains is listed above.

Email we send you

Your purchase email with the license key, and replies to your own support messages, are part of the service and are always sent.

If you have a Prep Sarthi pass, we may also email you once shortly before it ends. If you save your interviews, that email includes a summary of them: how many you did and how your practice scores moved.

We may also email you occasionally about Interview Sarthi and our other products, such as ApplySarthi. Every one of those carries an unsubscribe line: use it, or reply asking us to stop, and we send no more. We never sell or share your address.

Your rights

Email us to see or delete what we hold about you: your order, your email address, if you gave one your phone number, any Prep Sarthi interviews you chose to save (you can also export or delete those yourself in the app), and everything in My Sarthi (your profile, connected PCs and kept Live interviews, which you can also delete yourself on the page). For card, UPI and netbanking records contact Cashfree Payments if you paid from India, or Dodo Payments if you paid from outside India or before 16 September 2026. Data on your own PC is under your control at all times.
