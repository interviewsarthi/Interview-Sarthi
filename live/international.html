See the answer panel during a call

Live hears the question through your computer’s audio and shows a suggested answer using your resume. You review it and decide what to say. Answers start appearing in about 1.5 seconds; connection, model and question affect timing.

The panel is hidden from supported Windows screen sharing. Check the capture method and test both screens before a call. Use live assistance where the interview rules permit it.

Try it before your interview

  1. Install on your Windows computer. Use the Microsoft Store button above, or download the direct installer. The direct installer can trigger a Windows security warning; see the installation walkthrough.
  2. Add your own Gemini key. Follow the key setup guide, paste the key in Live and use Test. Check Google’s availability for your location. Google’s usage limits and any API charges are separate from your Sarthi pass.
  3. Upload your resume. Add your actual projects and work experience so suggestions have useful context. Optionally paste the job description and a few real examples of your own. English, Hindi and Hinglish are supported.
  4. Run a practice call. Check audio capture, answer relevance and screen sharing with a friend before relying on your setup. The free allowance is 30 minutes in total.
