Stays hidden

They see your screen. Not your sarthi.

Live Sarthi's panel stays hidden from supported screen sharing on Windows 10 (2004+) and Windows 11. The result depends on how the screen is shared and your setup. See what the interviewer can see and test both screens before the interview.

  • No bot joins your call: it listens to your computer's own audio. No extra participant, nothing in the guest list, nothing for the interviewer to notice.
  • Hidden from supported screen share: in a full-screen or window share from Windows 10 (2004+) or 11, the overlay stays hidden in compatible setups with Teams, Zoom, Google Meet and other call apps.
  • Supported meeting recordings: when recording uses the same supported capture method, the panel is excluded. Check your recording settings.
  • Silent: it reads, never speaks. No audio goes into the call. Your mic never triggers an answer.
  • Fades to almost nothing: drag it anywhere, dim it with one key, keep it at the edge of your eye.

FAQ

Questions people ask before buying

Can the interviewer see it?

The panel is hidden from supported screen sharing on Windows 10 (2004+) and Windows 11. Capture methods and recording settings vary, so test your own setup first. A hidden panel does not establish whether assistance is allowed in your interview.

Why does Windows say “unknown publisher”?

Only on the direct .exe, because it is not code-signed. It is not a detection of anything. The simplest fix is to install from the Microsoft Store instead, where the warning never appears. On the .exe, click More info then Run anyway; the full walkthrough with screenshots is further up this page. You can scan the file on VirusTotal first if you prefer.

Do I need to pay for the AI separately?

You supply your own Gemini API key. Eligible models have a free tier, subject to Google quotas and availability. If you enable paid API use, Google charges separately from your software pass. Check your limits before a mock call.

How are software access and AI usage priced?

The software pass and AI usage are separate. You buy a fixed-duration pass and use your own Gemini key. Compare the total cost, platform and usage limits before choosing a tool.

Does it work for my field, or only for developers?

Any field. It reads your resume, works out your field and its vocabulary, and tunes the listener to it, marketing, finance, nursing, sales, mechanical, teaching. Coding-round features are a bonus for developers.

Can I add the job description and my own examples?

Yes. Setup has two optional boxes under your resume: one for the job description, one for your own examples, a short paragraph per real story (what happened, what you did, the result). Answers then use the role's words and tell your stories with their names and numbers, so you can tell your own story at a glance. When no story or CV line fits a question, the app offers an example set inside a real job from your resume and marks that card MADE-UP EXAMPLE, so you know to adapt it. Leave the boxes empty and the resume alone is used.

Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes. It hears the mix and answers in the same register, in Latin-script Hinglish so you can read it fast.

I have just one interview coming up, which pass?

If it is one call, the 2-Day Pass (₹99). But most interviews have 2 or 3 rounds on different days. The 1-Month Pass (₹299) covers all of them, and any other company you interview with this month, with unlimited use. If you expect a second round, buy the month.

What exactly is free?

30 minutes, with every feature on: hidden overlay, Hinglish, screen reading. Use it in one call or two. When the 30 minutes end, the app does not close. You still see what the interviewer says; only the answers stop. Paste a pass key in the same window and the answers come back. The free minutes are per computer and do not come back if you reinstall.

What if it does not work for me?

Try the free 30 minutes first, on the PC and call app you will use: they are the full product. If a pass you bought does not work on your PC, tell us within 2 days of purchase (Help in the app, or support@interviewsarthi.com from the address you paid with). If we cannot fix it with you, you get the full amount back. Refund policy.

What happens when my pass ends?

The app shows a renew screen. Buy a new pass, paste the new key, continue. Nothing auto-charges, ever.

Is there a refer-a-friend offer?

Yes. Every pass comes with an invite code. When a friend buys any pass with your code and activates it on their own computer, you both get a Free Interview Day: a separate key for 24 hours on one computer. It starts only when you activate it, so you can keep it for your next round; activate it within 30 days of getting it. It is for new customers, and there is no limit on how many friends you invite. Get your invite link.

Which systems?

Windows 10 (2004) and 11. Any call app that plays through your speakers. Use headphones so your own voice does not leak into the microphone channel.

Is my data safe?

Interview audio, CV context and anything you paste in setup go from your PC to Google using your Gemini key. Transcripts are stored locally; licensing and purchase records are handled by our services. Read the privacy policy for details.
