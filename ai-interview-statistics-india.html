AI and interview research in India: sources and limits

Useful research separates what was measured from what was assumed. This page collects source checks for Indian job seekers and explains which conclusions the evidence supports.

Source review: 15 September 2026 · Published by Interview Sarthi

These announcements establish availability. They do not measure how many candidates use AI in interviews, how well an assistant performs or whether employers permit it.

Hiring and salary figures need a defined population

Before quoting a fresher salary, identify the employer, job track, location, graduating batch, publication date and whether the figure is fixed pay or total compensation. An individual offer or one hiring drive does not establish a national average. Check the current employer notice and your offer documents.

Our company interview guides support preparation; their illustrative processes and salary examples are not a measured market dataset.

Prices are product facts, not market statistics

Our comparison with official sources records available public prices and review limits. Compare like-for-like periods and included usage. Do not convert a short pass versus a monthly subscription into a claimed performance or savings multiple for an entire job search.

What would make an interview benchmark useful?

  • Public test cases with permission to use the audio and resume material.
  • Device, network, app version and model settings recorded with each run.
  • Separate measurements for transcription accuracy, response latency and unsupported personal claims.
  • Repeated runs with failures included, plus language and platform breakdowns.
  • A downloadable dataset and a clear disclosure that the publisher builds Interview Sarthi.

We have not published measured comparative results. No success rate, detection rate or accuracy ranking is claimed here.

How to cite and correct this page

Cite the original source for a third-party finding, and preserve its year and geographic scope. This page's editorial methodology may be cited separately. Send corrections and source documents to support@interviewsarthi.com.

Earlier unsourced aggregate claims and currency comparisons were removed during this review rather than presented as established statistics.
