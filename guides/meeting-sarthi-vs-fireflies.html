Meeting Sarthi vs Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is a note-taker with a meeting bot and a Chrome extension that records without it. Meeting Sarthi helps you answer during a client meeting and never joins it.

Updated 12 November 2026 · 5 min read · Published by Interview Sarthi, makers of Meeting Sarthi (help in client meetings on Windows)

Short answer: choose Fireflies.ai for recorded, searchable meeting notes for your team. Choose Meeting Sarthi for help answering the client while the meeting is on: live captions and a short reply from your own project.

Fireflies.ai details were read on Fireflies’ pricing page and its article on bot-free recording on 12 November 2026. We build Meeting Sarthi.

What Fireflies.ai says it does

Fireflies’ pricing page names Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans; the page we could read did not show their prices. It describes a Fireflies Notetaker bot that joins meetings, a Chrome extension that records “without Fireflies bot”, a desktop app, and “Real-time notes & live transcriptions”. In its own article it describes how a meeting bot works: “A separate account dials into your call and appears as ‘Notetaker’ in the attendee list,” and notes that “Some companies block them”.

Where Fireflies.ai is ahead

  • Recordings, transcripts and notes your whole team can search, with links into other tools.
  • More ways to run it: in the browser, a Chrome extension and a desktop app.
  • The AI is included in its plans; Meeting Sarthi needs your own free Google Gemini key.

Where Meeting Sarthi is different

  • It never joins the meeting, so nothing is added and no company blocks it at the door.
  • A short reply from your own project when the client asks something, during the meeting, and it never invents dates, names or numbers.
  • Projects, notes, documents and transcripts stay on your computer.
  • Captions and recaps are free; live answers are paid once, never renewing.

Be open about it

Whichever tool you use, follow your workplace’s and your client’s rules on AI tools, and ask for consent where the law requires it to record or transcribe a meeting.
