How do I prepare for an interview from the job description?

Turn every requirement into a question and match it to a real example from your work. Then practise those answers out loud. Prep Sarthi can run a voice mock interview built from your CV and the job description; the first 7 minutes are free.

Updated 12 November 2026 · 5 min read · Published by Interview Sarthi, makers of Prep Sarthi (voice mock interviews from your own CV)

Step 1: read the job description as a list of questions

Each line in “requirements” or “responsibilities” is a question the interviewer is likely to ask. “Experience with REST APIs” becomes “tell me about an API you built”. “Works with clients” becomes “tell me about a difficult client conversation”.

Step 2: match each one to your own example

For every requirement, write one example from your own work: what the situation was, what you did, and what changed. If you have no example for a requirement, prepare an honest answer about how you would learn it.

Step 3: use the description’s own words

If the description says “stakeholders”, say stakeholders. Using the same words shows the interviewer you read the role carefully and makes your fit easier to see.

Step 4: find what the description leaves out

Team size, how success is measured, what the first months look like: prepare two or three questions of your own about what the description does not say.

Step 5: practise out loud

Answer your list aloud and record yourself. Questions people ask

What if the job description is very short?

Use the job title, the company's other postings for similar roles and your own CV to fill in likely questions.

What if I don't meet every requirement?

Say so honestly and explain how you would learn it, with an example of something you learned quickly before.
