Is there an AI interview assistant for Zoom?

Yes. Live Sarthi is a Windows app that runs beside your Zoom interview, hears the interviewer’s question and shows a suggested answer from your own resume in about 1 second. It is free for 30 minutes on every PC; passes start at ₹149 for 2 days.

Updated 12 November 2026 · 4 min read · Published by Interview Sarthi, makers of Live Sarthi (help during live interviews on Windows)

How it works with Zoom

Live Sarthi is not a Zoom app and it does not join the meeting. It runs on your own Windows PC next to the Zoom window and listens to the call’s sound on your computer. You join the Zoom interview exactly as you normally would. The same app also works for Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and other call apps.

  • Answers from your own resume. It hears the interviewer’s question and shows a suggested answer in about 1 second, written from your resume, the job description and your own examples.
  • English, Hindi or Hinglish.
  • Hidden from supported screen sharing. Check it in a practice call first.
  • Unlimited interviews during a pass.

Get ready before the Zoom interview

  1. Install Live Sarthi from the Microsoft Store on a Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11 PC.
  2. Add your resume, the job description if you have it, and a few of your own examples.
  3. Add your free Google Gemini key with the two-minute guide.
  4. Test it in a Zoom meeting. Let the call play through this PC, check that suggestions appear, and share your screen once to check what the other side sees.

Sound on Zoom

Choose wired earphones or the laptop’s own speakers in Zoom’s audio settings, so the interviewer’s voice plays on the same PC as Live Sarthi. A call running on your phone cannot be heard by the app.

What it costs

Every Windows PC gets 30 minutes free, with no card. After that a pass is paid once and never renews: ₹149 for 2 days, or ₹299 for 30 days on 2 computers, with unlimited interviews during the pass. It uses your own free Google Gemini key. Use Live Sarthi only where the interview’s rules allow help; read what interview rules usually say about AI.

Questions people ask

Does Live Sarthi join my Zoom meeting?

No. It runs on your own Windows PC beside Zoom. Nothing is added to the meeting.

Do I need a Zoom plugin?

No. You join Zoom as usual and open Live Sarthi beside it on the same Windows PC.

Will the interviewer see it if I share my screen on Zoom?

Its panel is hidden from supported screen sharing. Check it in a practice Zoom call before the interview.

Which languages does it answer in?

English, Hindi or Hinglish.
