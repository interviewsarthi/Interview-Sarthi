Most AI meeting tools are note-takers: they join the meeting and send notes afterwards. A client meeting needs something else too: help with the question you have to answer now. Here is what each tool says about itself, Indian and international.

Updated 12 November 2026 · 7 min read · Published by Interview Sarthi, makers of Meeting Sarthi (help in client meetings on Windows)

Short answer: for team notes after every meeting, the note-takers (Otter.ai, Fireflies, Read AI, Fathom, tl;dv, Acta.ai) are built for that. For help during a client meeting without anyone joining, Meeting Sarthi is a Windows app that shows live captions and a short reply from your own project, with captions and recaps free.

Two kinds of tool

Note-takers. Otter.ai, Fireflies, Read AI, Fathom, tl;dv and Acta.ai are built to record and transcribe the meeting and give everyone notes, summaries and action items. Several join the meeting as an extra attendee, and some now also offer a way to record without one. If your team wants shared notes from every meeting, with search across them and links into other tools, they do that well, and they work in a web browser.

Help during the meeting. Meeting Sarthi is built for the moment the client asks something and you have to answer. It shows live captions, which helps when an accent is hard to follow, and a short reply from your own project: the notes and documents you confirmed. It never invents dates, names or numbers. After the meeting it drafts a recap with the to-dos for you to confirm.

Where the others are ahead

  • Team features. Shared workspaces, notes sent to everyone, and links into CRMs and other tools are the core of the note-takers. Meeting Sarthi is for one person in the meeting.
  • Where they run. Most work in a web browser through the meeting apps. Meeting Sarthi is a Windows app (Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11).
  • No key to set up. Meeting Sarthi uses your own free Google Gemini key; the others include their AI in the plan.

Where Meeting Sarthi is different

  • It never joins the meeting. Nothing is added to the meeting, so it works in whichever meeting app the client chose.
  • Help while the meeting is on: a short reply from your own project, not only notes afterwards.
  • Your work stays on your computer: projects, notes, documents and transcripts.
  • Paid once, if at all. Captions and recaps are free. Live answers: 30 free minutes on every computer, then ₹299 for 30 days on 2 computers, never renewing.

How to choose

  1. If you need notes for your whole team after every meeting, start with a note-taker’s free plan.
  2. If you need help answering the client during the meeting, try Meeting Sarthi’s free captions and its 30 free minutes of live answers in a practice call.
  3. Check your workplace’s and your client’s rules on AI tools, and ask for consent where the law requires it to record or transcribe a meeting.
