Is there an AI interview assistant for Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Live Sarthi is a Windows app that runs beside your Microsoft Teams interview, hears the interviewer’s question and shows a suggested answer from your own resume in about 1 second. It is free for 30 minutes on every PC; passes start at ₹149 for 2 days.

Updated 12 November 2026 · 4 min read · Published by Interview Sarthi, makers of Live Sarthi (help during live interviews on Windows)

How it works with Teams

Live Sarthi is not a Teams add-in and nothing joins your meeting. It is an app on your own Windows PC. You open Teams as usual, open Live Sarthi beside it, and it listens to the call’s sound on your computer. The same app works for Zoom, Google Meet and other call apps, so it does not matter which link the company sends.

  • Answers from your own resume. It hears the interviewer’s question and shows a suggested answer in about 1 second, written from your resume, the job description and your own examples.
  • English, Hindi or Hinglish.
  • Hidden from supported screen sharing. Check it in a practice call first.
  • Unlimited interviews during a pass.

Get ready before the Teams interview

  1. Install Live Sarthi from the Microsoft Store on a Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11 PC.
  2. Add your resume, and the job description if you have it, plus a few of your own examples.
  3. Add your free Google Gemini key. The key guide takes about two minutes.
  4. Do a practice Teams call. Play the call sound through this PC, check that suggestions appear, and share your screen once to check what the other side sees.

Sound on Teams

Live Sarthi needs to hear the interviewer, so the Teams call has to play on the same PC: through wired earphones or the laptop’s own speakers. If the call is on your phone, the app cannot hear it.

What it costs

Every Windows PC gets 30 minutes free, with no card. After that a pass is paid once and never renews: ₹149 for 2 days, or ₹299 for 30 days on 2 computers, with unlimited interviews during the pass. It uses your own free Google Gemini key. Use Live Sarthi only where the interview’s rules allow help; read what interview rules usually say about AI.

Questions people ask

Does Live Sarthi join my Teams meeting?

No. It is an app on your own Windows PC. Nothing is added to the meeting and nobody has to let it in.

Does it work in Teams on a phone?

No. Live Sarthi runs on a Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11 PC, and the Teams call has to play on that PC.

Can the interviewer see it when I share my screen?

Its panel is hidden from supported screen sharing. Check it in a practice call before the interview.

How much does it cost?

30 minutes free on every PC. Then a pass paid once: ₹149 for 2 days or ₹299 for 30 days in India ($9.99 or $29.99 elsewhere), with unlimited interviews during the pass.
