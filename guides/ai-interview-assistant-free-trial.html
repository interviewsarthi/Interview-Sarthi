Can I try an AI interview assistant for free?

Yes. Live Sarthi gives every Windows PC 30 minutes free, with no card, for help during a real interview. To practise first, Prep Sarthi’s first 7-minute mock interview is free, with no sign-up.

Updated 12 November 2026 · 4 min read · Published by Interview Sarthi, makers of Live Sarthi (help during live interviews on Windows)

Two free ways to start

Live Sarthi, 30 minutes free. A Windows app that hears the interviewer on Teams, Zoom, Google Meet or another call app and shows a suggested answer from your own resume in about 1 second, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. No card is needed for the free minutes.

Prep Sarthi, 7 minutes free. A voice mock interview in your browser, on a phone or a computer. The AI interviewer asks about your own CV and the job you want, follows up on weak answers and scores every answer with a stronger one.

Use the free minutes well

  1. Set up before you start the clock. In Live Sarthi, add your resume, the job description and your free Google Gemini key first.
  2. Test with a practice call. Play a call through the same PC and check that suggestions appear. Share your screen once to check what the other side sees: the panel is hidden from supported screen sharing.
  3. Close the app when you finish testing, so the rest of the free time stays for later.
  4. Practise out loud with Prep Sarthi if you want to rehearse answers before the real day.

After the free time

Live Sarthi: a pass paid once and never renewing, ₹149 for 2 days or ₹299 for 30 days on 2 computers, with unlimited interviews. Prep Sarthi: ₹99 for 30 days of unlimited mock interviews, paid once.
