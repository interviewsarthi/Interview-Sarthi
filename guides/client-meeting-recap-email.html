Client meeting recap email: template and example

A short recap after a client meeting turns what was said into what was agreed. It catches misunderstandings while they are still small, and it makes sure every action item has an owner.

Updated 12 November 2026 · 4 min read · Published by Interview Sarthi, makers of Meeting Sarthi (help in client meetings on Windows)

Short answer: Send it the same day. Keep it to one screen: the decisions, the action items (yours and the client’s) with one owner and one date each, the open questions, and the next meeting. End by asking the client to correct anything you got wrong.

What to include

  1. A clear subject line: “Recap and next steps: Payments API, 12 Nov”.
  2. One line of thanks and the purpose of the meeting.
  3. Decisions: what was agreed, in plain words.
  4. Action items: split into the client’s and yours. Each one gets one owner and one date.
  5. Open questions: what is still to be answered, and by whom.
  6. Next meeting: date and time, if one was agreed.
  7. A request to correct: “If anything here doesn’t match your understanding, please reply and I’ll fix it.”

Template

Subject: Recap and next steps: [project], [date]

Hi [name],

Thanks for your time today. A short recap of what we agreed:

Decisions
– [decision 1]
– [decision 2]

Your action items
– [task], by [date]

Our action items
– [task], [owner], by [date]

Open questions
– [question], [who answers], by [date]

Next meeting: [day, date, time]

If anything here doesn’t match your understanding, please reply and I’ll update it.

Regards,
[your name]

Example

Subject: Recap and next steps: Payments API, 12 Nov

Hi Rahul,

Thanks for your time today. A short recap of what we agreed:

Decisions
– Go-live stays on Friday 20 Nov, after the final staging check.
– Refund reports move to the next release.

Your action items
– Share the production IP ranges, by Tuesday 17 Nov.

Our action items
– Finish webhook retry testing, Priya, by Wednesday 18 Nov.
– Send the go-live checklist, Arjun, by Thursday 19 Nov.

Open questions
– Who signs off the go-live on your side? You to confirm, by Tuesday 17 Nov.

Next meeting: Friday 20 Nov, 10:00 IST

If anything here doesn’t match your understanding, please reply and I’ll update it.

Regards,
Arjun

Mistakes that cause trouble later

  • “The team will look into it.” No owner means nobody does it. Name one person.
  • No dates. “Soon” means a different day to everyone. Write the date.
  • Long paragraphs. Clients read recaps on their phone. Short lines and bullet points.
  • Sending it days later. By then, people remember the meeting differently. Send it the same day.
  • Internal notes in a client email. Keep your team’s private comments out of it.

Questions people ask

When should I send a client meeting recap?

The same day, while everyone still remembers the meeting the same way. A recap sent days later often starts a new discussion about what was said.

Who should get the recap?

Everyone who was in the meeting, plus anyone who has an action item in it.

What subject line should I use?

Something the client can find later, such as "Recap and next steps: [project], [date]".
