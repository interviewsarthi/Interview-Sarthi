Is Live Sarthi legit?

Yes — and you should not have to take our word for it. Live Sarthi gives every Windows PC 30 minutes free, with no card and no sign-up, so you can test it in a practice call before any money changes hands. The app installs from the Microsoft Store, passes are paid once and never auto-renew, and a first pass has a 7-day money-back promise.

Check it without trusting us

  1. Try before paying. 30 minutes free on every PC, no card, no sign-up; the phone web app gives 20 free minutes. Run a practice call with a friend and see the suggestions with your own eyes.
  2. Install from the Microsoft Store. The app comes from the Microsoft Store listing, not from an unknown download link, and updates arrive the same way.
  3. Read the plain-language pages first. The product facts, refund policy and prices are public, and Help answers before you pay too.

No subscription traps

In this category, most complaints that use the word “scam” are really about auto-renewal: a card quietly charged again, and no refund. Live Sarthi’s passes do not work that way. Every Windows PC gets 30 minutes free, with no card. After that a pass is paid once and never renews: ₹149 for 2 days, or ₹299 for 30 days on 2 computers, with unlimited interviews during the pass. You can pay by UPI in India, nothing is stored for a renewal, and there is nothing to cancel. A pass’s days start when you first activate its key, not when you pay, so buying today for next week’s interview wastes nothing.

What the interviewer sees

Nothing joins your meeting and nobody is invited into the call. The suggestion panel is hidden from supported screen sharing — and you should check that yourself in a practice call before the real one, because call apps change and every honest tool in this category owes you that sentence. The bigger truth: what people notice in an interview is behaviour, not software. Live Sarthi’s answers are drafted from your own resume, the job description and your own examples, so the words are yours to say naturally — it is a memory aid for your own experience, not an essay to read out.

Is it cheating?

That depends on the interview’s rules, and we say the same thing on every page: Use Live Sarthi only where the interview’s rules allow help; read what interview rules usually say about AI. Some interviews allow notes and references; some forbid help of any kind. Where help is not allowed, practise instead with Prep Sarthi, our mock interviewer — the first 7 minutes are free.

Questions people ask

Is Live Sarthi a real product?

Yes. It is a Windows app published in the Microsoft Store by Interview Sarthi, with public prices, a public refund policy and 30 free minutes on every PC before any payment.

Will money be deducted automatically later?

No. Passes are paid once and never renew: ₹149 for 2 days or ₹299 for 30 days in India ($9.99 or $29.99 elsewhere). Nothing is stored for a renewal and there is nothing to cancel.

Can the interviewer see the panel when I share my screen?

Its panel is hidden from supported screen sharing. Check it yourself in a practice call before the interview; the free 30 minutes exist for exactly that.

Kya ye app interview me sach me kaam karta hai?

Haan — par humari baat par mat jaiye. Har PC par 30 minute free hain, bina card ke. Ek practice call me khud dekh lijiye: answer aapke apne resume se bante hain, English, Hindi ya Hinglish me.

What if it does not work on my PC?

A first pass has a 7-day money-back promise, and the free 30 minutes let you test your exact PC and call app before paying. If anything is stuck, write to support@interviewsarthi.com.

Is using it cheating?

It depends on the interview's rules. Use it only where help is allowed; where it is not, practise beforehand with Prep Sarthi instead.

How much does Live Sarthi cost?

30 minutes free on every Windows PC, with no card. Then one-time passes: ₹149 for 2 days or ₹299 for 30 days on 2 computers in India ($9.99 / $29.99 elsewhere), with unlimited interviews during the pass.

Why is it so much cheaper than other interview assistants?

Because it runs on your own free Google Gemini key, we are not reselling AI time by the minute, and passes are one-time payments instead of subscriptions. The price is the real price.

Do I have to pay for an AI or API key on top of the pass?

No. Live Sarthi uses Google's free Gemini key, which you create in about two minutes with the site's guide. Google's free tier covers normal interview use; any paid Google usage would be between you and Google, not us.

Does it work with Zoom and Google Meet, or only Teams?

It works beside Teams, Zoom, Google Meet and other call apps alike, because it listens to the call's sound on your PC. Nothing joins the meeting, so it does not matter which link the company sends.

Does it answer in Hindi or Hinglish?

Yes. Answers can be in English, Hindi or Hinglish, drafted from your own resume and your own examples.

I have a MacBook. Can I still use it?

The Windows app needs Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11. With a Mac or any other laptop, use the phone web app: the interview runs on the laptop with its sound on the loudspeaker, and the suggestions show on your phone beside it. 20 minutes free, and one pass works in both.

Where do my resume and my interview go?

The interview audio and your resume context go from your PC straight to Google under your own free Gemini key — your account, not ours. Transcripts are stored on your computer, there are no analytics in the app, and our server only checks that your pass key is valid. The privacy policy spells out the data flows.

When do my pass days start: at payment or at first use?

At first use. The clock starts when you activate the key in the app, not when you pay, so you can buy today for an interview next week. A key has to be activated within 30 days of purchase.

What happens when the free 30 minutes end?

Answers stop until a pass is activated, and reinstalling the app does not reset the free trial. Nothing is charged by itself at any point: paying is always your own explicit step.
