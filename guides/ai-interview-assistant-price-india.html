How much does an AI interview assistant cost in India?

Live Sarthi costs ₹149 for 2 days or ₹299 for 30 days, paid once and never renewing, with unlimited interviews during the pass. Every Windows PC gets 30 minutes free first, with no card.

Which pass to pick

One interview, or a few rounds in two days: the 2-Day Pass. Several companies over the month, or interviews on two computers: the 1-Month Pass. Not sure yet: use the free 30 minutes in a practice call first.

What to compare before you pay

  1. Unlimited or credits. Some tools charge per minute or per credit, so a long interview day costs more.
  2. Paid once or a subscription. Check whether it renews by itself, and how to stop it.
  3. A real free trial. Try it in a practice call before you pay.
  4. Your interview language. Live Sarthi answers in English, Hindi or Hinglish.
  5. Your computer. Live Sarthi needs Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11.

What you get for the price

  • Answers from your own resume. It hears the interviewer’s question and shows a suggested answer in about 1 second, written from your resume, the job description and your own examples.
  • English, Hindi or Hinglish.
  • Hidden from supported screen sharing. Check it in a practice call first.
  • Unlimited interviews during a pass.
