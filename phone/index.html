How it works

Set it up once. Then just talk.

  1. 01

    Interview on loudspeaker

    Join your call on the laptop as you normally would. Play the sound on the laptop's speaker, not earphones.

  2. 02

    Phone where you can see it

    Open interviewsarthi.com/live/app, add your resume once, and prop the phone just below your webcam. Tap Start listening.

  3. 03

    Read it, say it your way

    When the interviewer asks something, an answer from your own projects and numbers appears in about a second. Your own voice stays quiet: it only answers the interviewer.

Webcam Your phone, just below it

The one trick

Put the phone where your eyes already are.

  • Just below the webcam. You read while looking at the camera, so it looks like eye contact.
  • Within an arm's length of the laptop's speaker, screen up, brightness up.
  • Do Not Disturb on, so a call or a notification can't take the microphone.

Questions

      
Before your interview

      
Is anything installed on my laptop?
No. Nothing is installed or opened on your laptop. Live Sarthi runs only in your phone's browser, so there is nothing on the laptop for a screen share or an app check to show.

      
Does it work with a Mac?
Yes. The interview runs on your Mac as usual and Live Sarthi runs on your phone next to it, so it works with a Mac, Windows, Linux or a Chromebook, and with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet or any other call app.

      
Can I use earphones?
No. Your phone has to hear the interviewer, so play the interview sound on the laptop's loudspeaker at a normal volume.

      
Where should I put my phone?
Where your eyes already go: propped just below your webcam is best, so you are looking at the camera while you read. Keep it within an arm's length of the laptop's speaker, screen up.

      
Which phones work?
Any recent Android phone or iPhone, and tablets, in Chrome, Safari or Edge. Nothing to install: open interviewsarthi.com/live/app and allow the microphone.

      
Does my Live Sarthi pass work here?
Yes. One pass works on the Windows app and on your phone. The phone does not use up the pass's computer slot.

      
Interviewing on a Windows PC? Live Sarthi for Windows keeps the answers in a panel your screen share doesn't show. Want to practise first? Prep Sarthi runs a real voice mock interview. Use interview assistance only where your interview's rules allow it.
