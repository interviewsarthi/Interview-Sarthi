What we could verify

Arya by Mentoria describes ATS-ready CVs tailored to each job, “the most relevant jobs from 40+ job portals”, AI job applications sent “straight to the recruiter’s inbox”, and automated follow-ups that are tracked when opened. Its plans: Free ₹0 (a limited-time trial with 15 AI-sent applications), Mini ₹299 for 7 days (100 applications), Pro ₹849 for 30 days (300) and Ultimate ₹2,899 for 3 months (1,350), each with automatic follow-ups. (Official pricing page and home page, read 12 November 2026.)

Its home page shows LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and Naukri only as logos in a graphic, and names “40+ job portals” without listing them, so we cannot say which sites it applies on.

Apply Sarthi is a Windows app that applies on Naukri, Foundit, Shine, LinkedIn, Indeed and company career pages. It fills each employer’s form in its own browser window on your PC and sends it, or a “Fill only” setting leaves the submit button to you. It is free every week (20 applications in the first week, then 15), with plans of ₹249, ₹499 or ₹799 for 30 days.

What should decide it

Arya’s strength is the follow-up: it sends applications to recruiters and follows them up for you, which suits people who want their CV in front of a person. Its plans are simple to read.

Apply Sarthi works inside the job sites themselves: it fills the employer’s own form on Naukri, LinkedIn and the rest, with the answers you saved, and keeps a safe daily pace on every site. If most of your applications go through those sites, that is the difference that matters. On price, its free weekly allowance never runs out, and 300 applications for 30 days cost ₹249 against ₹849 on Arya’s Pro.

How to test both properly

  1. Pick five real postings you would genuinely accept, and use the same five in both tools.
  2. Start from the same CV file in each.
  3. Read every tailored CV against your original, line by line. Any figure, date, employer or qualification that was not in your document is a reason to stop.
  4. Stop before submitting in whichever tool lets you, and compare the two filled forms field by field.
  5. Count fields left blank or filled wrongly, not how impressive the demonstration felt.

What we could not verify
