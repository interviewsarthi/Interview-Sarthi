Is ApplySarthi really free?

Yes. ApplySarthi is always free, with no subscription or card required. Public job browsing needs no Gemini key. CV reading can use a platform-provided key within usage limits; your first tailored CV is on us, and later tailoring and AI application help use your own Gemini key. Google’s usage limits and any API charges are separate.

Which job sites does ApplySarthi cover?

ApplySarthi brings together listings from sources including Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Foundit, Shine and Internshala, as well as company career pages and remote job boards. Coverage and availability vary by source. Always check the employer’s page for current details and location eligibility.

Does it automatically submit job applications?

By default, ApplySarthi fills the application in your own Chrome browser so you can review it and submit. Optional Autopilot can work through multiple jobs and submit only when enabled and nothing is left to check. You handle CAPTCHAs and any missing personal answers. Follow each job site’s rules and application limits.

Do I need a Gemini API key?

You can browse jobs without one. Initial CV reading can use a platform-provided key within usage limits. To tailor more CVs after your first free one, and to use AI application help, add your own Gemini key in Setup. Follow the Gemini key setup guide. Google’s quotas and billing are separate from ApplySarthi.

Can I use ApplySarthi on my phone?

Yes, you can browse jobs, view your matches and download tailored CVs on a phone. Application autofill needs the Chrome extension on a laptop or desktop. Start with a PDF or Word CV and sign in with your email.

Where are my CV and personal details stored?

Your CV, extracted profile, tailored documents and saved answers are stored in your ApplySarthi account on the server. Your Gemini key is stored encrypted. You can delete your account and its data from Setup. Read the ApplySarthi privacy policy for details.
