Is Apply Sarthi really free?

Yes, every week: 20 applications in your first week, then 15 every week, free, with no card required. For more, choose a plan in the app, paid once for 30 days through Cashfree (no auto-debit): Basic ₹249, Pro ₹499 or Max ₹799. Only applications that are sent count. The free job lists on apply.interviewsarthi.com need no account.

Which job sites does Apply Sarthi work on?

Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Shine, Foundit and company career pages. You sign in to the job sites you use, once, in the app's own browser window; company career pages need no sign-in. It keeps a safe daily pace on every site.

Does it automatically submit job applications?

Yes, when you choose "fill and send" (the default): it sends an application once every question is answered, and leaves the rest for you under "For you to finish". "Fill only" fills each form and waits for you to press Submit. You handle "are you human" checks yourself. It keeps a safe daily pace on every site. How automatic applying works, and what we never do.

Do I need a Gemini API key?

Yes, a free one from Google: the app's setup shows you how, and it takes about a minute. The app writes the answers on each form with it. Follow the Gemini key setup guide. Google’s limits are separate from Apply Sarthi.

Can I use Apply Sarthi on my phone?

Apply Sarthi is a Windows app, so it needs a Windows PC. On a phone you can still browse the free job lists.

Where are my CV and personal details stored?

Your CV, answers and applications are kept in your Apply Sarthi account so the app can apply for you, and your Gemini key is stored encrypted. Your job-site passwords stay in the app's browser on your PC. You can download your data or delete your account in Settings. Read the Apply Sarthi privacy policy for details.
