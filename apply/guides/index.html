Questions people ask

What is the difference between ApplySarthi and Interview Sarthi?

They cover the two halves of a job search. Apply Sarthi handles applying — it is a Windows app that finds open jobs, matches them to your CV, tailors the CV for each one, and fills and sends the application. Interview Sarthi handles what happens after: live, resume-grounded help during the interview itself, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Both run on the same free Google Gemini key.

Do I need to pay for ApplySarthi?

Not to start. Apply Sarthi is free every week: 20 applications in the first week, then 15 every week, on your own Google Gemini API key, which Google issues at no cost. For more, plans are paid once for 30 days: Basic ₹249, Pro ₹499 or Max ₹799. Interview Sarthi is a separate product with its own passes.
