Auto apply on Indeed India: what works, is it safe, what it costs

Yes, applications on Indeed can be filled and sent for you. Before you let any tool do it, read what Indeed’s own terms say about automation. This page covers both, plainly.

Published 11 November 2026 · Written by the team that builds Apply Sarthi

Short answer: Apply Sarthi is a Windows app that finds jobs on Indeed that fit your CV, fills each application with your details and your saved answers, and sends it. Its “Fill only” setting fills each form and leaves Submit to you. Free every week: 20 applications in the first week, then 15.

What “auto apply on Indeed” can mean

  • Indeed’s own quick apply. Indeed calls it Indeed Apply. Jobs that use it show an “Easily apply” badge, and you apply on Indeed itself with your Indeed profile (Indeed’s description). It is quick, but you still open each job and press the buttons yourself.
  • The employer’s own site. Other listings take you to the company’s career page, where you fill that company’s form.
  • A tool that fills and sends for you. This is what people usually mean by auto apply: software finds the jobs, fills the forms and submits them.

Is it safe? Read Indeed’s terms first

We would rather you hear this from us. Indeed’s terms for job seekers (last updated 29 September 2026) say that automating the Indeed Apply process with anything other than Indeed’s own tools is not allowed, and that Indeed may limit how many applications you can submit in a day (Indeed’s terms, “Applying to Jobs”, “Limits”). So any tool that sends Indeed Apply applications for you, ours included, is used at your own choice and at your own account’s risk. No tool can promise that an account will never be limited.

What you can control:

  • Use “Fill only”. Apply Sarthi then fills each form and waits; you read it and press Submit yourself.
  • Keep the daily number modest. Apply Sarthi keeps a safe daily pace on every site by itself.
  • Stay signed in as yourself. You sign in to Indeed in the app’s own browser window, the ordinary way. Apply Sarthi never stores your job-site passwords and never solves “are you human” checks; those wait for you.
  • Apply only where you fit. It applies to jobs that fit your CV, not to everything that matches a keyword.

How to start

  1. Get Apply Sarthi for Windows. It is being published on the Microsoft Store. Until the Store link is on the Apply Sarthi page, leave your email there and the link is sent to you.
  2. Add your CV once and say which jobs you want.
  3. Sign in to Indeed yourself inside the app’s own browser window.
  4. Choose “fill and send” or “Fill only”, then press Start applying.
  5. Open “For you to finish” now and then. A question only you can answer waits there.

The same app also applies on Naukri, LinkedIn, Shine, Foundit and company career pages, so one CV and one set of answers cover all of them.

Before you send the first one

  • Bring your Indeed profile and CV up to date; they go with your applications.
  • Check the first three applications by hand before trusting the fourth.
  • Indeed is one source among several. In our snapshot of 18 September 2026, our job data held 12,708 open postings from Indeed, and more than that from company career pages.
