How it works

Add your work. Take the call. Confirm the recap.

01 · Before

Add what you know

Type or say a quick note ("webhook check done, waiting on sandbox keys"), add your documents, or connect a folder. Notes the AI writes stay drafts until you confirm them. Press Prepare for likely questions with answers.

02 · During the call

Start the call panel

When someone asks you something, the reply appears in a second or two, with the note or document it came from. Missed one? Press help me answer.

03 · After the call

Confirm the recap

Status changes, decisions, action items and commitments arrive as drafts, with a follow-up message you can copy. Tick what is true and it joins your notes, ready for the next call.

What it will not do

It answers from your work, not from guesses.

It never invents progress.

A reply may only state a date, name, number or commitment from your confirmed notes or your documents, and it shows which one. With nothing to cite it suggests "I'll confirm that and get back to you today."

It never joins the meeting.

No bot, no invite, no extra participant. It listens to your computer's sound and your microphone, so it works in Teams, Zoom, Meet, Webex or anything else.

It never sends anything.

Recaps, follow-up messages and notes are drafts until you confirm them. Documents, folders, Git, GitHub and Jira are only read.

Your work stays on your computer.

Projects, notes, documents and transcripts are saved on your computer and your saved keys are encrypted for your Windows account. Call audio goes to Google Gemini on your own key; we never receive your calls.

Questions people ask

Short answers.

What does it cost?

Every new computer gets 30 free minutes of help on real calls, and practice calls are always free. After that a pass is Rs 99 for 30 days on two computers, paid once by UPI, card or netbanking. It never renews by itself. There are no refunds once you have bought, so try the free minutes first.

Does a bot join my meeting?

No. Nothing joins the call and nobody is invited. The app listens to the sound your computer plays and your microphone, so it works with Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Webex and any other call app, including calls someone else hosts.

Where does the call audio go?

From your computer to Google Gemini on your own API key, so it can be understood. Your projects, notes, documents and transcripts are saved on your computer; we never receive them. We see licence checks and the problem reports you choose to send. A free Gemini key lets Google use what is sent to improve its products, so for client calls use a key with billing turned on, and follow your workplace's rules on AI tools.

Can it invent a status?

It is built not to. A reply may only state a date, name, number or commitment that is in your confirmed notes or your documents, and the panel shows where it came from. When they do not answer the question, it suggests a polite "I'll confirm that and get back to you today" instead of guessing.

Which documents can it read?

PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, PowerPoint and text files, or text you paste. Connect a folder, including a OneDrive or Google Drive folder synced to your computer, and it re-reads a file when it changes. Git, GitHub and Jira can be read too. Everything is read-only.

Does the other side see the panel?

If you share the screen it is on, yes. Share a single window, or keep the panel on another screen. Always tell the people on the call that you use an AI assistant that listens.

Hinglish?

Replies are in plain English, because that is what you will say on the call. Voice notes can be spoken in Hinglish.

How is this different from Interview Sarthi?

Interview Sarthi helps a candidate during a job interview, from their CV. MeetingSarthi helps on work calls, from your own project notes and documents. Separate apps, separate passes: a key for one does not open the other.
