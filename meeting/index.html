SHORT ANSWERS

What you need to know.

What you need
  • A Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11 computer you are allowed to install software on.
  • A Google Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com, pasted once in Settings. How to get one. For client meetings, turn billing on for it.
  • Optional: a free Groq key, so notes and recaps use it and your Gemini key is kept for the call.
  • Your workplace’s and your client’s permission to use an AI tool on calls, and the consent of the people on the call.
What does it cost?

Captions and the recap on your real calls are always free, and so are notes, documents, Prepare for call, Ask and practice calls. Live answers during a call get 30 free minutes on every new computer; after that a pass is Rs 299 for 30 days on two computers ($29.99 outside India), paid once. It never renews by itself. There are no refunds once you have bought, so try the free minutes first.

Does a bot join my meeting?

No. Nothing joins the call and nobody is invited. The app listens to the sound your computer plays and your microphone, so it works with Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Webex and any other call app, including calls someone else hosts.

Where does the call audio go?

From your computer to Google Gemini on your own API key, so it can be understood. Your projects, notes, documents and transcripts are saved on your computer; we never receive them. We see licence checks and the problem reports you choose to send. A free Gemini key lets Google use what is sent to improve its products, so for client meetings use a key with billing turned on, and follow your workplace's rules on AI tools.

Can it invent a status?

It is built not to. A reply may only state a date, name, number or commitment that is in your confirmed notes or your documents, and the panel shows where it came from. When they do not answer the question, it suggests a polite "I'll confirm that and get back to you today" instead of guessing.

Which documents can it read?

PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, PowerPoint and text files, or text you paste. Connect a folder, including a OneDrive or Google Drive folder synced to your computer, and it re-reads a file when it changes. Git, GitHub and Jira can be read too. Everything is read-only.

Do I need to tell the people on the call?

Yes. Tell the people on the call that you use an AI assistant that listens: in many places, and under many company and client rules, you need their consent. Meeting Sarthi shows this notice once when you first start it.

Hinglish?

Replies are in plain English, because that is what you will say on the call. Voice notes can be spoken in Hinglish.

How is this different from Interview Sarthi?

Interview Sarthi helps a candidate during a job interview, from their CV. Meeting Sarthi helps in client meetings, from your own project. Separate apps, separate passes: a key for one does not open the other.
