A month of unlimited use: how the prices compare

In India, ₹299 buys 30 days of unlimited live interview help on two computers, paid once. Every paid monthly plan of the international tools above costs more than ₹299. Checked 12 November 2026.

Many tools sell hours instead of days. For one or two interview days, Live Sarthi's 2-day pass is ₹149 with no hour limit; CrackInterviewAI's ₹349 buys 3 hours and Cluegent's ₹499 buys 3 hours.

What Live Sarthi offers for the price

  • 30 minutes free on every Windows PC, no card.
  • ₹149 for 2 days or ₹299 for 30 days on 2 computers, unlimited interviews, paid once, no renewal ($9.99 or $29.99 outside India).
  • Answers in about 1 second, written from your own resume, the job description and your own examples.
  • English, Hindi and Hinglish.
  • Works with Teams, Zoom, Google Meet and other call apps. The panel is hidden from supported screen sharing; check it in a practice call first.
  • Uses your own free Google Gemini key. Use it only where the interview's rules allow help.

Where another tool may suit you better

  • Free time every day: InterviewPilot's free tier, 3 sessions of 10 minutes a day.
  • A computer that does not run Windows, or a phone: Final Round AI, Cluely, Verve AI, Parakeet AI, LockedIn AI, OphyAI and AssistBee list apps or browser versions beyond Windows. Live Sarthi is a Windows app only.
  • Mock interviews in the same tool: Verve AI, Final Round AI Pro and Parakeet AI include practice interviews. For Live Sarthi users, practice is a separate app, Prep Sarthi.

Test before a real interview

A price table does not measure answer quality. Use each tool's free time on the same mock call: ask a question about one of your projects, a follow-up, and a mixed Hindi-English sentence with a number in it. Check that answers match your resume and do not invent facts. Read the rules guide before using any assistant in a real interview.

Questions people ask

Is there a free AI interview assistant?

Most tools give a free trial rather than a free plan: Live Sarthi gives 30 minutes per PC, CrackInterviewAI 30 minutes and Cluegent 12 minutes. InterviewPilot's free tier gives 3 sessions of 10 minutes every day, and Verve AI's free plan gives 3 live sessions of 15 minutes.

Which interview assistant answers in Hindi or Hinglish?

Live Sarthi answers in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Of the other tools here, AssistBee mentions transcription in 10+ languages; the others' pricing pages do not mention Hindi or Hinglish.

Do I need a subscription?

No, not for every tool. Live Sarthi, CrackInterviewAI and Cluegent's hour packs are paid once and do not renew. Final Round AI, Cluely, Verve AI and OphyAI list monthly or yearly plans.
