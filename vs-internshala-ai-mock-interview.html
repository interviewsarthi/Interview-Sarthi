Who each one suits

Internshala suits you if 5 free mock interviews a month are enough and you want to review yourself on video.

Prep Sarthi suits you if you want to practise more than 5 times, speak without a camera, or start in a phone browser without signing up. After the free demo, ₹99 gives 30 days of unlimited mock interviews; after buying, you add your own free Google Gemini key.

Our verdict

Use Internshala's free mocks, and add Prep Sarthi when you need more practice before a specific interview.

Questions people ask

Is Internshala's AI mock interview free?

Yes, 5 full mock interviews a month, per its page (checked 12 November 2026). Its page does not state a paid price.

Does Prep Sarthi need a camera?

No. Prep Sarthi is a spoken interview in the browser.

Which one uses my CV?

Prep Sarthi builds questions from your CV and the job you want. Internshala personalises its questions to a job description.
