Live Sarthi vs Ghotly AI

Both are Windows apps that help during live interviews and sell passes that do not renew. Ghotly AI costs less for a month; Live Sarthi offers Hindi and Hinglish answers, a longer free trial and a 2-day pass.

Who each one suits

Ghotly AI costs less for a month: ₹79 against Live Sarthi's ₹299, and it covers up to 3 PCs. If price for a whole month is what matters most, it is the lower number.

Live Sarthi suits you if you want answers in Hindi or Hinglish as well as English (Ghotly AI's page does not mention languages), longer free testing (30 minutes per PC against 10 minutes per account), or a ₹149 pass for 2 days. Answers are written from your own resume, the job description and your own examples, and start in about 1 second. It uses your own free Google Gemini key.

Our verdict

Try both free tiers on the same practice call before paying. Choose on how well the answers match your resume and your language, not only on price. Use any assistant only where the interview's rules allow help.

Questions people ask

Is Ghotly AI cheaper than Live Sarthi?

For a month, yes: Ghotly AI Pro is ₹79 for a calendar month (checked 12 November 2026); Live Sarthi is ₹299 for 30 days on 2 PCs. For one or two interview days, Live Sarthi also has a ₹149 pass for 2 days.

Which one has a longer free trial?

Live Sarthi gives 30 minutes on every Windows PC with no card. Ghotly AI gives 10 minutes per account.

Which one answers in Hinglish?

Live Sarthi answers in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Ghotly AI's page does not mention languages.
