A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you choose.

Four apps, each with a different job.
Use the one you need today.

What does InterviewSarthi do?

InterviewSarthi offers four apps. Apply finds jobs on multiple sites and applies for you from a Windows app. Prep runs spoken mock interviews and gives feedback. Live shows answer suggestions during interview calls on Windows. Meeting helps in client meetings from your own project.

Can I prepare for a job I found in Apply?

Yes. Bring that job's description and your CV into Prep to practise for the role. You can add your CV to Live too. The apps do not automatically sync your documents.

Does Live appear in a screen share?

Live hides its answer panel from supported screen capture on Windows 10 (2004+) and Windows 11. Test your meeting setup before your call. This does not cover every recording method. Use assistance where the interview rules permit.

How fast does Live show answers?

Answers start appearing in about 1 second. This is an approximate start time, not a promise that the complete answer arrives in that time. Your model, connection and question affect speed.

Can I try the apps before paying?

Apply Sarthi is free every week (20 applications in the first week, then 15 a week). Prep includes a free 7-minute demo interview. Live includes 30 minutes once per device. Meeting Sarthi's captions and recaps are free, with 30 free minutes of live answers per computer. These are total free allowances, not new minutes for every session. Prep and Live passes are purchased separately.

What do I need to use them?

Apply works in a browser; personal matching needs sign-in, and the Apply Sarthi Windows app applies for you by itself. Prep needs a browser and a microphone; its free demo needs no key, and a pass uses your own free Gemini key, set up after you buy. Live needs Windows 10 (2004+) or Windows 11 and your Gemini key. Google's limits and any paid AI usage are separate.
